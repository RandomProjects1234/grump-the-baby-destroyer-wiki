Wiki / The Toddlers

The Toddlers

The other children left behind. Finding them is optional; keeping them alive is what most of the score is actually made of.

Names

There are 10 possible names, cycled through as more toddlers spawn over a run: Pip, Moo, Bibi, Tog, Nell, Dot, Wex, Bun, Cricket, Snib.

Spawning

Every morning, a fresh batch scatters into rooms away from your classroom: clamp(2 + floor(night/2), 2, 5) — 2 on nights 1–2, rising to a cap of 5 from night 7 onward. They never spawn behind the staff room's locked door, in the boiler room, in your own classroom, or in a hallway. Any toddler still marked "taken" from the previous night is removed first, so the count each day is always the fresh batch, not an accumulation.

Hunger

Each toddler has its own hunger stat, 0–100, rising at 1.5 per second normally (0.75/second if it's been given a teddy and calmed), doubled on a Hungry night modifier. Past 65% hunger, an uncalmed toddler starts crying periodically — a real noise event, not just a visual cue.

Crying

A crying toddler emits noise every 6 seconds if lost (out in the building) or every 9 seconds if already safe in your classroom, at noise level 0.9 (lost) or 0.5 (safe) — both loud enough for Bob or Grump to potentially hear, which is the actual cost of leaving one hungry. Calming a toddler (giving it a teddy) suppresses this entirely for as long as the calm status holds.

Picking up and carrying

  • Your hands must be free (no fuel can, teddy, or other toddler already held) to pick one up — a 0.45-second hold.
  • Carrying a toddler slows your movement more than any other big item: speed ×0.76 (versus ×0.87 for a fuel can, teddy, or hamster).
  • A carried toddler rides along with you and doesn't need to be "aimed" — it just follows your position.
  • If you go down while carrying one, it's released back onto the floor at your position rather than vanishing or staying stuck to you.

Feeding and calming

  • Feed a hungry one (over 40% hunger, with any food item in your bag): a 0.7-second hold, resets its hunger straight to 0 and its cry timer to 20 seconds. Worth +15 score and progresses the "Snack time for the little ones" job.
  • Give it a teddy: an 0.8-second hold while holding a teddy in your hands. Marks it calm permanently for that toddler — halves its hunger gain rate and raises its cry threshold effectively to never (since !calm gates crying entirely). Worth +45 score.

Getting them safe

Carry a toddler into your own classroom and put them down (E, or automatically detected on entry) to mark them safe. This is worth +120 score the first time, plays a ding sound, and a toast confirms it by name. Putting one down anywhere else just sets them back to "lost" on the floor there.

Safe isn't risk-free

If Grump (turned) catches you, there's a 70% chance he also drags one already-safe toddler back out of your classroom as an extra cost — another −100 score, on top of whatever the catch itself did to you. See Grump.

Being taken at nightfall

The instant night falls, every toddler still marked lost (not carried home, not already safe) is taken by Grump, one at a time, off-screen: −80 score each. If any were taken, a big-line banner reads "[names] is not here any more" with a stinger sound. Getting every toddler either safe or actively being carried before dusk is the only way to prevent this — there is no saving a lost toddler once night has actually begun.
