Wiki / Mrs. Honeywell

Mrs. Honeywell

Your teacher, and the one grown-up in the building who is actually on your side.

Her routine

She stands at the chalkboard in your own classroom every morning, reading out the day's list in a recorded voice line: "Can you get all these quests done for me? You can go home at the end of the day, but look out for Grump. He's kind of a special one." She lives entirely on each player's own machine — her spot and her routine follow the school layout and the phase clock, so nothing about her needs to be sent over the network in co-op. She disappears 25 seconds before the day phase ends, and nobody sees her go.

Talking to her

Talk to her and she cycles through a rotating list of lines while showing the objectives list:

  • "Off you go, then. The list will not do itself."
  • "Stay busy, little one. Busy is safe."
  • "Anything you finish, I will leave something in the crib for you."
  • "Keep the lights on tonight. Promise me."
  • "Mind the boy in the green dungarees. Be polite. It will not help, but be polite."
  • "If you hear Bob humming, find somewhere small to be."
  • "Eat something. You cannot be brave on an empty tummy."
  • "Meredith in the cafeteria will give you a tray of lunch. Keep the beat for her."
  • "If Jerry comes running with that whistle, just do what he says. It is quicker."

She notices a dark classroom

If your own classroom is unlit (light switch off, or the generator not running) and you've been standing in it for more than 45 seconds into the day, she'll tell you about it herself — "Hey, make sure there's electricity, okay? It's kind of dark in here" — with a 60 second cooldown between nags. Talking to her directly while it's dark triggers the same line immediately. See Events & Encounters for the exact conditions.

Voice acting

She has 2 of the game's 13 recorded voice lines: the morning announcement and the "it's dark in here" line. See Voice Lines.
