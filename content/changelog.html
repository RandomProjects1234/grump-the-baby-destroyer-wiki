Changelog

A summary of the game's own commit history, in plain language. This wiki tracks whichever commit is named in the footer of every page — when the game changes, this wiki may lag until it's rebuilt against the new commit.

CommitDateSummary
213863d2026-09-13Doors work while carrying a toddler. Picking up a kid used to replace every prompt with "Put them down", so no door could be opened and no light switch used until you set them down again — it looked like doors (such as the boiler room's) randomly refusing to open. Doors, light switches and the exit doors now work with a toddler in your arms. While looking at a door, R puts the kid down; anywhere else, E or Q does. The only doors that ever stay shut are the staff room (needs a staff key), the front doors (until you've survived your nights) and the playground doors at night.
560ec3f2026-09-13You can drop bag items. Q used to only put down big things held in your hands (fuel cans, spare parts); items in your bag could not be dropped at all. Now, with empty hands, Q drops the item in your selected hotbar slot (pick one with 1–6). Dropped items land in front of you only where there is open floor, otherwise at your feet, so they can never end up inside a wall or a desk. Dropping your only torch switches it off. You cannot drop things while hiding.
89a410a2026-09-13Bug fixes: co-op jobs, stuck teachers, cutscene safety. A joiner getting lunch from Meredith (or passing Jerry's gym class) never reached the host, so joiners could not finish the "Lunch from Meredith" job — fixed. Grump and Bob can no longer catch a player who is in a minigame or the Big Boys Club cutscene. Jerry could stay frozen mid-class forever, and Meredith could stay stuck "serving" and never serve again, if a minigame was cut short — both fixed. Meredith now waits if Jerry is already running at you; Jerry gives up if the card boy turns up and disappears if the Big Boys Club scene starts. Drop, torch, R and hotbar keys no longer work during the cutscene (M and Tab still do). If Esc released the mouse during a minigame, the pause menu now appears afterwards so you can click Resume.
78b81f52026-09-12Jerry the gym teacher, Meredith the lunch lady, and minigames. Added Jerry (red tracksuit, whistle) who runs at you during the day and forces one of three pop-up 2D minigames — Laps, Dumbbells, or Jump Rope (see Minigames). Added Meredith (hairnet, apron), who stands at the cafeteria counter all day and gives you a tray of lunch after a three-lane rhythm minigame, tray quality scaling with accuracy. Both appear on the map with legend entries. New "Lunch from Meredith" job on Mrs. Honeywell's list. Nothing hunts a player while they're in a minigame; solo pauses the world for it, co-op does not. Fixed a crash where silent sound effects threw a Web Audio error. The game now has 13 recorded voice lines (was 10).
f23bc482026-09-12Big Boys Club cutscene: Grump stands up for you. Added a one-per-run cutscene, triggered on day 2 or 3 before Grump turns: three big kids from the top year corner you in a hallway, shove you, and mock Grump — who walks up, says "Why you being mean?", grows, and puts all three on the floor before they run off shouting "We're telling Mrs. Honeywell!" Letterboxed, camera-locked, local to each player. Lowers the real Grump's resentment by 20 and adds +50 score. See The Big Boys Club.
5735ca22026-09-12Searching always works. Any searchable container can now be searched again and again the same day; each repeat search finds less (a normal roll on the first search, then a 45% / 20% / 9%… chance to keep whatever the roll finds on repeats). Aiming at furniture now checks the whole box from any side instead of one fixed point in front, so odd-shaped furniture like sandpits and lunch tables works from any angle. Holding E now keeps its original target, so a kid or Grump walking through your view no longer resets the hold bar. Toddlers, dropped items and messes sitting in front of furniture now correctly take the interaction prompt over the furniture behind them. In co-op, two players can search the same container at once. See Searching.
df20bce2026-09-12Fixed friends walking backwards, short quest lists, Grump spawning in furniture. Co-op teammates' models were rotated 180° from the direction they were actually walking. Mrs. Honeywell's daily job list could come up short a job if it picked a job template that needed a room type the generated school didn't have (jobs now skip unavailable rooms while the list is being planned, instead of being dropped afterwards). Grump's morning wander spot and his "he is closer" night-start spot could land inside furniture or somewhere unreachable; both are now required to be clear of furniture and have a walkable path to your classroom, and the "closer" placement can no longer land him inside your own classroom.
a84a5b62026-09-12Map, Mrs. Honeywell, power and quest fixes. Added Mrs. Honeywell as a real character (stands at the chalkboard, reads the day's list aloud, warns about a dark classroom) and a new minimap/full map. Fixed the generator wearing out about 6× too fast and stalling constantly, a prompt bug that hid the starter cord behind "Repair" when carrying a part, playground doors that never reopened after night 1, room-search jobs that could target rooms without enough to search, staff-room jobs needing a key that shouldn't have been required, light-switch jobs being impossible without power, and several co-op/prompt/UI bugs.
d530adb2026-09-12Fix co-op, add daily quests and food, more nights, scarier Grump. Rewrote co-op so joiners' actions (fuel, repairs, cleaning, feeding, drops, Grump answers) actually reach the host instead of only existing on the joiner's own screen; fixed the host being unable to see joiners, damage always landing on the host, and several other multiplayer bugs. Added Mrs. Honeywell's daily job list with crib rewards, a hunger/food system, a 3/5/7/10/15/20/Endless night-count choice, difficulty settings, night twists from night 5, and a meaner turned Grump (charges on sight, camps hiding spots, pops bulbs, relocates, quotes your answers, jumpscares).
d17832c2026-09-11Corrected the voice-line count quoted in the game's own README.
f6269102026-09-11Bob owns the nights; Grump turns on day 4. Pinned down exactly who is dangerous when: Bob patrols every night from night 1 on every difficulty and gets faster each night; Grump now turns on a fixed schedule (dawn of day 4) rather than purely on resentment, with a maxed resentment meter only able to bring that forward, never delay it.
005e5dc2026-09-11Initial release. Grump the Baby Destroyer: a 3D survival-horror school game built with Three.js and PeerJS, fully static with no build step. The school is a pure function of a seed, so co-op only ever needs to send one number.
