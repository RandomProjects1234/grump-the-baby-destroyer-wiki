The Day

Days are for work: search the school, feed the generator, clean up, feed and carry the other toddlers home, and get through Mrs. Honeywell's list.

The day loop

At the start of each day: the previous night's messes are cleared and a fresh batch spawns, every toddler still "taken" from the previous night is removed and a new batch is scattered around the school, the day's job list is drawn up, and 2–3 lunchboxes appear (the count is 2 + (night % 2), so it alternates between 2 and 3). Every prop in the school is reset to unsearched.

Day length depends on difficulty — see Nights & Difficulty for the table. With 30 seconds left in the day, the game warns you: "IT IS GETTING DARK."

On day 1 specifically, one fuel can and one spare part are pre-placed near the boiler room generator, so the very first day always has a way to get the lights on.

Mrs. Honeywell's list

Mrs. Honeywell herself is at the chalkboard in your classroom every morning, reading the day's list out loud in a recorded line the moment the day begins, and she'll go over it again if you talk to her. See Characters for what else she does — including telling you off if your own classroom is sitting dark.

Every morning the list is redrawn. Day 1 is always the same fixed three jobs, to teach the loop: get the generator started, eat something, and rescue a toddler. From day 2 onward the list is drawn from a shared pool, weighted so the "big three" (fuel, food, toddlers) come up more often than flavour jobs; the crayon job also only ever appears before Grump has turned.

The list has 3 jobs per day, rising to 4 jobs from night/day 6 onward. Every job finished leaves its reward sitting in the crib, back in your (home) classroom.

Reward pools

When a job completes, its reward is picked from a pool keyed by tier, and the items are placed in the crib in your classroom.

Food and hunger

Your tummy (food stat) drains at 0.25 per second — roughly one full meal's worth over a complete day-and-night cycle, per the game's own comments. Below 25% you get a "your tummy hurts" warning; at 0% you start losing health at 1.6 per second until you eat or go down.

Eating also knocks 6 points off your fear meter. Food lives in the cafeteria, kitchen and staff room (search their furniture), and 2–3 lunchboxes (each holding 2–3 random food/drink items, 40% chance of 3) spawn around the school every morning in the cafeteria, library, gym, classrooms, playground, music or art rooms — press R to open one.

Between jobs

Outside of active jobs you'll want to: search containers for fuel, parts and food; keep the generator running (see The Generator & Power); clean up messes before dusk, since messes left overnight make the generator burn fuel faster; and talk to Grump if he's around — ignoring him for a whole day adds +6 resentment at nightfall on top of the base nightly amount, which is more than most single answers cost him.
