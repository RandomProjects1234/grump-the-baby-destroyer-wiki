Food & Hunger

Your tummy (food stat), what refills it, and what happens when you let it run out.

How hunger works

Food drains at a flat 0.25 per second — by the game's own comment, roughly one full meal's worth over a complete day-and-night cycle. There is no separate "hunger" stat for the player — it's the same 0–100 food number throughout.

Food levelEffect
Below 25%A one-time warning toast: "Your tummy hurts. Find something to eat." (re-arms once you climb back over 40%)
Below 20%Movement speed ×0.82
Below 15%Fear rises an extra +1.5/second, on top of the normal rate
0%You lose 1.6 health per second until you eat or go down

Health regenerates slowly during the day (+0.9/second, only while not downed and food isn't at zero), which is one more reason to fix your food before dusk rather than during the night.

Eating (R with food selected) also knocks 6 points off your fear meter, on top of restoring food — a small but real reason to eat proactively rather than only when desperate.

Where food comes from

  • Search containers in the cafeteria, kitchen, and staff room — see Loot Tables for exact odds per container type. Vending machines are the single richest food source (snack/juice only, but a combined ~44–72% chance depending on the night).
  • Lunchboxes: 2–3 spawn loose around the school every morning (2 + night % 2, so it alternates between 2 and 3), in the cafeteria, library, gym, classrooms, playground, music, or art rooms. Open one with R for 2 items (60% chance) or 3 (40% chance), randomly drawn from sandwich/apple/milk/snack/juice. If your bag is full, the extras spill onto the floor next to you.
  • Meredith's lunch line: one tray a day, quality scaling with a rhythm minigame. See Meredith and Minigames.
  • Mrs. Honeywell's job rewards: several reward pools include food. See Mrs. Honeywell's Jobs.
  • Feeding toddlers spends your food items on them, not the other way around — see The Toddlers.

Food-related jobs

"Lunch time" (eat 1–2 things), "Snack time for the little ones" (feed a hungry toddler), and "Lunch from Meredith" (get a tray) are all drawn from Mrs. Honeywell's job pool — see Mrs. Honeywell's Jobs for the full templates.

Fear interaction. Because low food both slows you down and speeds up your fear gain, letting your tummy run dry compounds every other night-time problem at once — see Fear and crying.
