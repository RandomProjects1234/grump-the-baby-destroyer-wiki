Wiki / Voice Lines

Voice Lines

Everything in the game is procedural — synthesised footsteps, breathing, ambience — except 13 recorded voice lines. This page is every one of them: who says it, the audio file, and exactly what makes it play.

Recorded vs. synthesised. Grump's ambient mutters (see Grump's Questions) and every one-off sound effect (dings, thuds, stingers) are synthesised with Web Audio, not recorded. Only the lines below are actual voice recordings.

Grump — 3 lines

FileInternal idPlays when
audio/grump-greeting.ogggreetingThe instant you open a dialogue with him (before he's turned) — every single time, regardless of stage.
audio/grump-mean.oggmeanHis reply after you answer a question, if his stage is below Hostile (3); an ambient line when stage is 1–2; the line "That is mine. You touched it." when you return his crayon; his spoken line during the Big Boys Club cutscene ("Why you being mean?").
audio/grump-angry.oggangryHis reply after you answer once stage 3+; ambient lines at stage 3+ and once turned; the stage-up reveal sound; a charge; catching you (the jumpscare); refusing to talk once turned.

Bob — 3 lines

FileInternal idTranscript / trigger
audio/bob-class.oggbobClass"GET BACK TO YOUR CLASSROOM." — plays the moment he spots and starts chasing someone.
audio/bob-scream.oggbobScreamA scream, on a successful grab/catch.
audio/bob-mess.oggbobMess"These darn kids, leaving the lights on and making a mess." — on a 35-second cooldown, when he personally switches a room's lights off while patrolling, or walks within ~3.2 units of an uncleaned mess.

Mrs. Honeywell — 2 lines

FileInternal idTranscript / trigger
audio/honeywell-morning.ogghoneywell"Can you get all these quests done for me? You can go home at the end of the day, but look out for Grump. He's kind of a special one." — plays at the start of every single day, the instant the list is up.
audio/honeywell-dark.ogghoneywellDark"Hey, make sure there's electricity, okay? It's kind of dark in here." — when your classroom is unlit and you've lingered 45+ seconds, or when you talk to her directly while it's dark. 60-second cooldown between nags.

Jerry — 2 lines

FileInternal idTranscript / trigger
audio/jerry-laps.oggjerryLaps"Are you slacking off? Three laps around this gym, now!" — when he picks the Laps minigame.
audio/jerry-2.oggjerryLift"Yeah, strength! Lift those dumbbells — it's dumbbell time! Let's go, almost there! Let's go! W, you did it!" — when he picks the Dumbbells minigame.

The Jump Rope minigame has no recorded line — Jerry instead gets a synthesised whistle tone plus the text cue: "Jerry: *FWEEEET* 'Jump rope! Six in a row! Nobody leaves until I see six!'"

Meredith — 1 line

FileInternal idTranscript / trigger
audio/meredith-lunch.oggmeredith"Here's your reward for being so good at your quests. Have some lunch, kid!" — when she hands you your tray at the end of the lunch-line minigame, whatever your score was.

The Card Kid — 2 lines

FileInternal idTrigger
audio/kid-line.oggkidLineWhen he begins showing you his card collection (its length sets how long the Talk phase of the encounter lasts — minimum 3.4 seconds).
audio/kid-scream.oggkidScreamThe moment the Taker drags him away. This same file is reused for the Big Boys Club leader's cry when Grump lands the first hit on him.
